### 📌 Business Problem
A record label wants to know:
1. What makes a global hit on Spotify? (Song, Artist, Genre, Year)
2. Which country produces top artists?
3. When was peak streaming and why decline after 2019?
4. Which genres to invest in for 2025?

### 📂 Dataset Overview
- **Source:** Spotify Global Music Success (1985-2024)
- **Total Streams:** 252.46 Billion
- **Top Artist:** Ed Sheeran
- **Top Genre:** POP
- **Best Year:** 2019 (peak streaming year)
- **Metrics:** Total Streams (in Billions), Genre Streams, Artist Streams, Release Year

### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel - Advanced Interactive Dashboard
- **Excel Skills:**
    - Pivot Tables for Top Songs/Artists/Genre aggregation
    - Slicers: Artist (24kGoldn ft. iann d..., Adele, Ariana Grande, Benson Boone), Genre (Acoustic Pop, Afrobeats/Pop, Alt Rock, Art Pop), Year (1985, 1991, 1994, 1996)
    - Custom KPI Cards with sparkline trends
    - Bar Chart (Top streamed songs), Pie/Donut (Most streamed genre), Line Chart (Streaming trend overtime), Treemap (Top Artist's Country), Column Chart (Top 5 Artists)
    - Navigation buttons (OVERVIEW, ARTISTS, GENRE, TRENDS, INSIGHTS)
    - Branding & Color Theme: Spotify Dark Green #1DB954 style

### 📈 Key Insights & Results - Overview Page

**KPIs:**
- **Total Streams:** 252.46B
- **Top Artist:** Ed Sheeran
- **Top Genre:** POP
- **Best Year:** 2019

**1. Top Streamed Songs in Billions - Blinding Lights Dominates:**
1. Blinding Lights - **~5.3B** (Highest)
2. Shape of You - ~4.8B
3. Someone You Loved - ~4.2B
4. Sunflower - ~4.0B
5. One Dance - ~3.9B
6. Stay - ~3.7B
7. Believer - ~3.6B
8. Rockstar - ~3.5B
> **Insight:** The Weeknd's Blinding Lights is the most streamed song globally - 10% more than #2.

**2. Most Streamed Genre in Billions - Pop is King:**
- Pop: **43.97B (35%)**
- Indie Pop: 29.49B (23%)
- Hip-Hop: 17.93B
- Synth-Pop: 16.54B
- Alt Rock: 15.93B
- Pop Rock: 12.8B
> **Insight:** Pop + Indie Pop = 58% of all streams. Pure Pop still drives global success. Investment should be 70% Pop variants.

**3. Top 5 Artists by Stream in Billions:**
- Ed Sheeran: **~15.5B** (Clear leader)
- Harry Styles: ~13.0B
- The Weeknd: ~11.0B
- Imagine Dragons: ~8.9B
- Taylor Swift: ~7.2B
> **Insight:** Ed Sheeran dominates by 19% over #2. British artists (Ed + Harry) = 28.5B combined.

**4. Streaming Trend Overtime in Billions (1985-2024):**
- 1985-2012: Flat <5B (Pre-streaming era)
- 2013-2014: Jump to 10B (Spotify global expansion)
- 2015-2019: Explosive growth to **~40B in 2019 peak**
- 2020-2022: Decline to ~15B (Post-COVID, TikTok competition, market saturation)
- 2023-2024: Slight recovery to ~10B
> **Insight:** 2019 was the golden year. Post-2020 decline suggests algorithm changes & short-form video competition.

**5. Top Artist's Country in Billions - USA Dominates:**
- USA: **125.86B (49.8%)** - Half of all streams
- UK: **49.16B (19.5%)**
- Canada: 25.12B (9.9%)
- Australia, USA/UK Collabs, South Korea: Rest ~20%
> **Insight:** USA + UK = 69.3% of top artist streams. Anglo market dominance is extreme. Afrobeats/Pop is emerging but still small (see slicer).
